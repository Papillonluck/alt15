What is Travel Performance?

Timing wellness to each guest’s journey means connecting the rooms, the food, the spa and the gym, which sit across different departments, so currently no one owns it across the hotel. That’s what ALT‑15° does, using AI and circadian rhythm science, personalised and bespoke to each guest.

It starts with what the hotel already knows. The booking shows where each guest has come from, how many time zones they’ve crossed and what time it is for their body on arrival. A short questionnaire adds whether they’re a morning or evening person and the purpose of the trip. Before arrival, each guest receives a bespoke plan: when to eat, train, recover and sleep across the stay. They can accept or change any part of it, and the hotel has everything arranged and ready for when they arrive.

Over six months, ALT‑15° designs your hotel’s protocol and signature packages using circadian science and AI, with our PhD jet lag specialist, embeds them across your departments and trains your teams. Your own teams deliver and sell them, designed to lift spend per guest, guest satisfaction and repeat stays.

For your hotel, that means wellness guests actually use, and a stay they come back for.

Helping guests feel well is what separates a hotel from its competitors.

The traveller

Every journey has a reason.The hotel decides whether they deliver it.

Business, a corporate event or a personal occasion. Whatever the reason for the trip, the hotel is part of a much bigger journey. How the guest feels throughout the stay shapes how well they can deliver on the trip’s purpose.

That starts with understanding why they are there, how little time they have, how the journey has affected their sleep, energy and recovery, and what they need from the stay. It ends with an experience designed around the traveller and the purpose of their trip.

Get that right, and your hotel becomes what guests remember long after their stay, the first they think of when they return to that destination, and the reason they come back.
